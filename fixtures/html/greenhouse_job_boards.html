Field Service Technician

Remote, United States

Ridgeline Robotics builds autonomous floor scrubbers for warehouses and airports. We are hiring a Field Service Technician to install, repair, and maintain our fleet at customer sites across the Midwest.

What you will do

  • Install and commission robots at new customer sites
  • Diagnose mechanical, electrical, and sensor faults on site
  • Train customer staff on daily care and basic troubleshooting

What we are looking for

  • 3+ years servicing industrial or commercial equipment
  • Comfort with multimeters, wiring diagrams, and hand tools
  • Willingness to travel up to 60% of the time
