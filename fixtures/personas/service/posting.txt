Front Desk Agent, Bluffview Inn & Suites

Bluffview Inn & Suites is a 120-room hotel on the Savannah riverfront. We are hiring a Front Desk Agent for evening and weekend shifts.

Responsibilities:
- Check guests in and out and handle reservations by phone and email
- Answer guest questions and resolve complaints at the desk
- Balance the cash drawer at the end of each shift
- Work with housekeeping to keep room status current

Requirements:
- 1+ year in customer service, hospitality, or retail
- Comfortable with a property management system (Opera PMS experience a plus)
- Available evenings, weekends, and holidays
