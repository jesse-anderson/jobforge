Regional CDL-A Driver, Heartland Produce Express

Heartland Produce Express hauls refrigerated produce across 8 states in the central US. We are hiring regional Class A drivers who are home every weekend.

Responsibilities:
- Haul refrigerated loads on regional routes, 2,500 miles a week on average
- Complete pre-trip and post-trip inspections
- Log hours of service on ELDs
- Communicate with dispatch and receivers on delivery windows

Requirements:
- Valid Class A CDL with a clean MVR
- 2+ years of Class A experience
- Current DOT medical card
- Hazmat endorsement a plus
