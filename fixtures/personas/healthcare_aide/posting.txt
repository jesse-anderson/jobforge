Patient Care Technician, St. Brendan Medical Center

St. Brendan Medical Center is a 300-bed hospital in Lubbock, TX. Our medical-surgical units are hiring Patient Care Technicians for 12-hour night shifts.

Responsibilities:
- Assist patients with bathing, feeding, mobility, and toileting
- Take and record vital signs, intake and output, and blood glucose checks
- Perform phlebotomy and 12-lead EKGs as trained
- Report changes in patient condition to the RN

Requirements:
- Active Texas CNA certification
- Current BLS certification from the American Heart Association
- 1+ year of direct patient care
- Phlebotomy certification or EKG Technician certification preferred
