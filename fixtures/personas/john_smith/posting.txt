Operations Supervisor, Prairie Freight Co.

Prairie Freight Co. runs 6 cross-dock facilities across central Illinois. We are hiring an Operations Supervisor for our Springfield site to lead a second-shift team of 20.

What you will do:
- Lead receiving, picking, and shipping on second shift
- Own order accuracy and on-time dispatch targets
- Build schedules that match inbound volume
- Run daily safety briefings and keep the site injury-free

What we are looking for:
- 3+ years supervising warehouse or distribution teams
- Experience with a warehouse management system (WMS) and RF scanning
- Forklift certification, or willingness to get it
- Strong Excel skills
- SAP experience is a plus
