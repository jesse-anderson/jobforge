Mechanical Design Engineer, Ironwood Fluid Systems

Ironwood Fluid Systems designs centrifugal pumps for municipal water and oil and gas customers. We are hiring a Mechanical Design Engineer for our Tulsa engineering team.

Responsibilities:
- Design pump components in SolidWorks and release drawings with GD&T
- Run FEA and hydraulic performance analysis
- Work with suppliers and the shop floor on castings and machined parts
- Support testing on our 2,000 hp test loop

Requirements:
- BS in Mechanical Engineering
- 2+ years of design experience
- SolidWorks and FEA experience
- EI certificate; PE license preferred
- ANSYS Fluent or other CFD experience a plus
