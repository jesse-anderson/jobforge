Project Coordinator, Harbor Construction Group

Harbor Construction Group builds and renovates commercial interiors across the South Sound. Our project managers need a Project Coordinator to keep jobs on schedule and on budget.

What you will do:
- Coordinate subcontractors, deliveries, and site schedules
- Track budgets, change orders, and invoices
- Keep owners and project managers updated

What we want:
- 3+ years coordinating people, vendors, or schedules
- CAPM or PMP preferred
- Smartsheet or similar scheduling tools
- Construction experience is a plus but not required
