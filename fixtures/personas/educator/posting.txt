Science Instructional Coach, Coburg Ridge School District

Coburg Ridge School District serves 9,000 students in 14 schools near Eugene, OR. We are hiring a Science Instructional Coach to support middle and high school science teachers.

Responsibilities:
- Coach teachers through observation, co-planning, and feedback cycles
- Lead district work on Next Generation Science Standards units and assessments
- Plan and run professional development for science departments
- Support new teachers and student teacher placements

Requirements:
- Oregon teaching license with a science endorsement
- 5+ years of classroom science teaching
- Experience leading curriculum work or mentoring teachers
- National Board Certification preferred
